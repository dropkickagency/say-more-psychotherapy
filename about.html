Our Vision

To be a trusted home for honest, unhurried psychotherapy — where people feel safe enough to say what hasn't been said, and meet themselves with more curiosity than judgement.

Our Mission

To support individuals across Ontario in achieving mental and emotional balance through tailored, trauma-informed therapy that meets you where you are — in person in Brampton or virtually anywhere in the province.

🌿 Services

Mental Health Services.

A complete range of services to support mental well-being — therapy, consultations, and crisis support, working together to help you recover, regulate, and reconnect.

Individual Therapy

Personalised one-to-one sessions with a licensed therapist to address your unique mental health needs.

Couples & Relationships

For conversations that keep looping and the ones you've been afraid to have. Interracial and intercultural couples welcome.

Online Counselling

⏰ Work Process

Our healing approach.

At Say More, we believe in a holistic and personalised approach to mental health care — one that moves at your pace, not the symptoms'.

01

Initial Consultation

A free 15-minute call to understand what you're carrying and what kind of support might fit.

02

Personalised Plan

We shape the work around your story, your pace, and the modalities that suit you best.

03

Therapeutic Sessions

Regular weekly or biweekly sessions, in person in Brampton or virtually anywhere in Ontario.

04

Progress Review

We check in regularly to make sure the work is doing what it should — and adjust where it isn't.

05

Ongoing Support

What's been said in the room becomes something you can carry into the week. We stay alongside as long as you need.
