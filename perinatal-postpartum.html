About The Therapy

The road to and through parenthood is rarely linear — and the cultural script often misses the harder parts. Whether you're walking through infertility, miscarriage, IVF, a difficult pregnancy, or postpartum depression and anxiety, the work is unhurried and honours every part of the story.

Paras pays close attention to the medical context as well as the emotional one — the appointments, the hormones, the silence around loss — so you don't have to translate or explain everything twice.

Is this right for you?

  • You're trying to conceive and it's taking longer than expected
  • You've experienced pregnancy loss, miscarriage, or infant loss
  • You're pregnant and the feelings are more complicated than "happy"
  • You're postpartum and don't quite recognise yourself
  • You want a therapist who understands the medical and emotional terrain

❓ Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

If yours isn't here, send a short note — we'll get back to you within two business days.

Can my partner come too? ⌄
Yes — joint sessions are available if it would help. Many clients also book solo sessions in parallel.
I haven't been diagnosed with PPD — is this still for me? ⌄
Yes. You don't need a diagnosis to come. Difficult feelings in pregnancy and postpartum are common and worth attending to.
Can we start before the baby arrives? ⌄
Absolutely. Many clients start earlier — sometimes during fertility treatment, sometimes early in pregnancy.
What if I've experienced loss? ⌄
The room makes full space for that. We move at your pace and honour the grief, however it shows up.
