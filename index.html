Get To Know Your Therapist.

Paras Geramian

Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) · CRPO

Paras is a relational psychotherapist working in Brampton and across Ontario. Her practice draws from psychodynamic, mindfulness-based, and somatic traditions — slowing things down enough to notice the patterns shaping your life, often before you knew their names. She works often with people sitting at the edges of cultures, languages, and identities. The room is warm, honest, and unhurried — you set the pace.

🤝 Benefits

The Benefits of Say More Psychotherapy.

A trauma-informed, relational practice that draws from psychodynamic, mindfulness-based, and somatic modalities — without leaning on jargon.

  • Warmth, Not Performance
  • Plain Language For Hard Things
  • Confidential, Considered, Secure
  • Culturally Aware, Identity Affirming
  • Trauma-Informed Throughout

What to expect.

We'll meet on a call for about 15-20 minutes. You don't need to prepare anything — and you don't need a label, diagnosis, or referral to come.

  • A short, friendly conversation — bring whatever's on your mind.
  • A chance to ask questions about how we work, fees, and what insurance might cover.
  • No pressure to book a session afterward.
  • If we're not the right fit, we'll point you somewhere that might be.
  • Completely confidential and CRPO-aligned from the very first minute.
