How it works

  • Text: click any heading or paragraph → type → click outside.
  • Images / videos: click → pick a new file. Videos max 4 MB.
  • Backgrounds: hover a section → click Change background. Some sections have a dark overlay for text contrast — it fades while editing but stays on the live site so the text remains readable.
  • Save & Publish: writes your changes and puts them live on the website. Visitors see it on their next page load.
