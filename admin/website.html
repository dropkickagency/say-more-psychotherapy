How it works

  • Text: click any heading or paragraph → type → click outside.
  • Images / videos: click → pick a new file. Videos max 4 MB.
  • Backgrounds: hover a section → click Change background.
  • Save: stores drafts. Not visible on the live site.
  • Publish: marks everything on this page as live. Visitors see it immediately.
  • New page: pick a starter layout, name it, and it'll appear in the nav.
