Get Your Free 15 Min. Session

Take 15 minutes to see if we're a fit. No commitment, no pressure — just space to ask questions, share what's been on your mind, and find out what working together might look like.

What to expect.

We'll meet on a call for about 15-20 minutes. You don't need to prepare anything — and you don't need a label, diagnosis, or referral to come.

❓ Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

About the free consultation — and what happens after. Anything else, send a short note.

Is it really free? ⌄
Yes. The 15-minute consultation is offered without charge and without obligation. You won't be invoiced and you won't be pressured to book a session afterward.
What actually happens in those 15 minutes? ⌄
A short, friendly conversation. You can share whatever's on your mind, ask questions about how we work, fees, and what insurance might cover. We can also talk through whether ongoing work makes sense — or whether someone else might be a better fit.
What if I'm not sure I want therapy yet? ⌄
That's a fine reason to come. The consultation is meant for exactly this — a low-stakes way to see how the room feels before deciding anything.
Is the consultation virtual or in person? ⌄
Virtual, on a secure video call. If you'd rather meet in person before committing to ongoing work, mention it in the form and we'll arrange a Brampton visit.
What happens after I send the form? ⌄
You'll hear back within two business days with a few time options. The reply comes directly from Paras — not an auto-responder or scheduling tool.
Do I need a referral or a diagnosis? ⌄
No. You can come for any reason — or none yet. Many people start the consultation without quite being able to name what's bringing them in.
